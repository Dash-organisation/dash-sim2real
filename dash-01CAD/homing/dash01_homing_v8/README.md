# dash01_homing_v8: init pose of DASH-01 with the relocated achilles (xshape)

Same role as `../dash01_homing_v7`: **qpos = 0 is the init pose (kneeling)**, so normalized 0° on
every motor = this pose. Until 2026-11-17 the six drives were zeroed here; they are now zeroed
STANDING (the `EXPORT_assembly-v8_closed.xml` pose, `STAND_POSE_DEG`), and the capture records that
pose's normalized angles, so normalized 0° still means this pose (see
`controller/fixed_gait/webui/calibration.py`). Signs and conventions are unchanged
(see the v7 README §1–§2); only the achilles linkage moved.

| file | what |

## 2. What changed from v7 (checked mesh by mesh)

| part | v7 → v8 | geometry | frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| body, hips, cams, pushrods, tibias | same | identical | identical |
| thigh | `thigh_*-v3` → `thight_*-v2/v4` | new part (achilles anchor moved) | knee pivot identical: 350.00 mm, same direction to < 0.001° |
| foot | `foot-v4` → `foot-v2` | new part (achilles anchor moved) | different frame |
| achilles | 512 mm → 492 mm between pins | new part | — |

Achilles pins: on the thigh at (−24.5, ·, −290) mm in the thigh frame (v7: (0, ·, −415)); on the foot
at (24, ·, −70) mm in the new foot frame.

Joint names changed (Fusion renumbered them). Motor joints: `left.thigh` = **Revolute-43**,
`right.thigh` = **Revolute-40** (v7: 33 / 27). abd (3 / 2) and cam (38 / 30) unchanged. Passive:
knee L-47 / R-41, ankle L-45 / R-39, achilles L-46 / R-42, pushrod L-37 / R-29. Axes of the six
motor joints are the same as v7, so `twinmap.DEFAULT_SIGNS` and `calibration.DEFAULT_SIGNS` stay.

The exporter spelled the thigh bodies `thight`; `derive_init.py` renames bodies and geoms back to
`thigh` (mesh files keep the exported name) so the twin's `/thigh_left/` regexes match.

## 3. How the init pose was derived

Hip, cam and thigh frames are identical between v7 and v8, so "same motor angles" means: same
orientation of each motor body relative to its parent as in `dash01_homing_v7` qpos 0. From the
export pose that is (qpos, deg): abd L +25.000 / R −25.000, cam L −55.472 / R +55.251,
thigh L +17.110 / R −16.841. The passive joints are then solved by continuation from the export
(knee loop, then achilles loop), staying on the CAD assembly branch; the result is baked into the
body `pos`/`euler`. Loops at qpos 0: < 1e-8 mm.

Result compared with v7 init:
- hips, cams, pushrods, thighs, tibias: same pose (< 0.001°, < 0.02 mm);
- feet: folded ~11° further back (foot long axis −43° vs −32°), lowest foot point at z −341 mm
  vs −316 mm;
- the kneeling contact is still the distal end of the thighs at z −432 mm; the achilles no longer
  comes near the ground (v7: −424 mm), feet are 91 mm above it.

## 4. Single-motor ranges from the init pose (kinematic only)

Each motor alone from init, others at 0, until a loop cannot close (no collisions, no stops):

| motor | normalized range (deg) | limited by |
|---|---|---|
| abd | ±25 (sweep cap) | — |
| cam | −5 … +75 | knee loop both ends |
| thigh | −53 … +4 | knee loop both ends |

v7 for comparison: cam −5 … +47, thigh −31 … +4, the upper end limited by the achilles loop.
The achilles loop no longer limits anything in single-motor sweeps.

## 5. Standing pose: the v36 pose does NOT stand flat on v8, so Home moved to the v8 export pose

With the current `daemon.STAND_POSE_DEG` (abd −25, cam +53.09 / +52.97, thigh +16.78 / +16.71),
v8 stands on its toes: sole contact only at x = 151…157 mm, heel 27 mm above the floor, foot
pitched ~5° nose-down. The CAD export pose of v8 is flat:

| side | abd | cam | thigh |
|---|---|---|---|
| left | −25.00 | +55.47 | +17.11 |
| right | −25.00 | +55.25 | +16.84 |

Soles flat at z −877 mm, x −90…+189 mm; CoM x +39 mm, y +0.2 mm (CAD masses 5.32 kg, motors
not included). **This is `daemon.STAND_POSE_DEG` since 2026-11-09** (🏠 Home goes there).
